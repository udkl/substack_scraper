# RAMACO RESOURCES (+36.48%)

I owe you a candid review on Ramaco Resources, a position that does not appear in our current holdings but shaped my thinking on risk management.

Three lessons from this experience:

1. **Due diligence on management is not optional.** We failed to dig deeply enough into the backgrounds of key executives. This will not happen again.
2. **When a short report lands, the first question is not “is it true?” but “does it matter?”** Even if Wolfpack’s claims prove exaggerated, a cult of negativity forming around a small cap stock can become self fulfilling. Capital has opportunity cost; fighting a narrative war is rarely worth the headache.
3. **A 36% gain is still a gain.** It is tempting to frame this as a failure because we “lost” 74 percentage points of paper profit. But we entered with a thesis, the thesis partially played out, and we exited with a positive return. The lesson is not that we failed; it is that we could have succeeded more decisively with better process.

Ramaco reminded me that markets are humbling. I would rather bank a 36% win and learn a lesson than hold through a potential zero trying to prove a short seller wrong.
