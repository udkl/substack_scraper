### Business Description

Perpetua Resources is a gold and antimony development company focused on its Stibnite Gold Project in Idaho, USA. The project boasts a world-class reserve of 4.8 million ounces of gold and 148 million pounds of antimony. The company is currently pre-production, with its business model centered on developing and operating the Stibnite mine to produce gold and antimony. A key aspect is its "restoration mining" approach, where proceeds from mining fund a comprehensive environmental cleanup of a historic brownfield site. The company aims to transition from a developer to a fully funded, construction-ready producer.

### Investment Thesis

**Upside:** Price target of $28.50 (or $27.50 for 110% return)implies significant upside.

The project's after-tax NPV is $3.7 billion at recent spot prices ($2,900/oz gold, $21/lb antimony) compared to the current EV of $1.24 billion, indicating a massive valuation disconnect. Further upside from potential increases in gold and antimony prices.

**Range of Outcomes:** Robust $1.4 billion after-tax NPV at conservative consensus prices ($2,100/oz gold, $10/lb antimony) provides a strong base case.

**Downside Protection:** The project's economics remain viable at lower commodity prices (15.4% IRR at $2,100 gold). Strategic importance of antimony as the only domestic US source provides a geopolitical moat and government support. Final federal permits have been secured, de-risking the project significantly.

### What would a short case look like?

> Major Cost Blowout: Initial CAPEX of $2.2 billion could escalate dramatically due to unanticipated inflation or construction issues, leading to highly dilutive equity financing.
>
> Financing Failure: Inability to secure the large EXIM debt facility, creating a massive funding gap and jeopardizing the project timeline.
>
> Commodity Price Crash: A severe and prolonged bear market in both gold and antimony would compress margins, reduce NPV, and negatively impact investor sentiment and debt servicing ability.
>
> Successful Legal Challenge: Despite securing permits, legal challenges from environmental or anti-mining groups could cause costly delays or halt construction.

#### Porter’s Five Forces

**Supplier Power:** 8/10

* *Comment:* The mining industry relies on a concentrated group of specialized engineering firms, equipment manufacturers, and skilled labor, giving suppliers significant pricing power.

**Customers**: 2/10

*Comment:* For gold, Perpetua will be a price taker in a global, liquid market. For antimony, given the critical domestic shortage and lack of substitutes, buyers (e.g., DoD) will have limited bargaining power, likely affording Perpetua premium pricing.

**Barriers to Entry and Exit:** 9/10

*Comment:* Very High. Discovering a world-class deposit is rare, followed by a multi-year, capital-intensive process of drilling, engineering, and permitting (Perpetua spent 8+ years and hundreds of millions).

**Threat of Substitution:** 1/10

*Comment:* Very Low. There is no financial or industrial substitute for physical gold as a monetary asset. For its key defense applications, antimony trisulfide is considered essential and non-replaceable.

**Competitive Intensity of Industry:** 7/10

*Comment:* High, but Differentiated. The gold development space is crowded, but Perpetua stands apart due to its high grade, low-cost profile, advanced permitted status, Tier-1 jurisdiction, and strategic antimony co-product.

**Overall Comment:** 27/50

*Comment:* The industry structure is generally favorable for Perpetua due to very high barriers to entry, very low threat of substitutes, and low buyer power, despite high supplier power and moderate competitive intensity.

### Competitors & Competitive Advantage

As a pre-production company, Perpetua currently has no market share. Upon production, it will be a significant US gold producer and the *only* domestic source of antimony, giving it a unique strategic position. The Stibnite Gold Project is a world-class, high-grade, low-cost open-pit gold project. Its most unique feature is the large antimony co-product, designated as critical to national defense and clean energy, providing a geopolitical moat. The "restoration mining" model also differentiates it. Sources and trajectory of competitive advantage:

> Geological Rarity: The Stibnite deposit is an exceptionally rare combination of high grade, large scale, and suitability for low-cost open-pit mining.
>
> The Permitting Barrier: Successfully navigating the complex, multi-year U.S. federal permitting process (8 years, hundreds of millions spent) creates a significant, durable barrier to entry for competitors.
>
> Its status as the only domestic source of a critical, non-substitutable defense mineral provides unique geopolitical and economic leverage, including direct government support and funding. Evidence of pricing power: For gold, Perpetua will be a price taker. For antimony, given the critical domestic shortage and lack of substitutes, it is likely to command premium pricing from strategic buyers like the Department of Defense.
>
> Perpetua possesses a strong and durable economic moat based on the geological rarity of its asset, the significant permitting barrier it has overcome, and the unique strategic importance of its antimony co-product.

### Revenue & Cost

> Pre-production, so no current revenue. Future revenue will be highly dependent on gold and antimony production volumes and prevailing commodity prices.
>
> Projected average of ~463,000 ounces of gold per year for the first four years from a high-grade starter pit. Antimony production will also contribute significantly.
>
> Highly sensitive to gold and antimony prices. Gold is in a secular bull market with forecasts of $3,675-$4,000/oz by 2025-2026. Antimony prices have surged due to supply disruptions and China's export ban, with recent quotes significantly higher than consensus.
>
> Revenue will be generated from both gold and antimony sales, with antimony providing a crucial by-product credit that significantly lowers gold's All-In Sustaining Cost (AISC).

#### Cost Structure

**Cost structure / margin summary:** Initial capital expenditure (CAPEX) is estimated at $2.2 billion. The project boasts exceptionally low All-In Sustaining Costs (AISC) of below $760/oz over the life of the mine, and a remarkable ~$435/oz in the first four years of production, placing it in the first quartile of the global cost curve. This low AISC is primarily driven by the significant by-product credit from antimony production.

### Industry & Operational Overview

**Cyclicality**: Both gold and antimony markets are cyclical, but currently in bull phases. Product Lifecycle: Gold is a mature asset, while antimony is a critical mineral with increasing strategic importance. Industry Structure: Gold mining is fragmented. The antimony market is highly concentrated, with over 90% of mined supply controlled by China, Russia, and Tajikistan. Supply: Gold supply is stable. Antimony supply is in a severe deficit, exacerbated by China's export ban to the U.S. Demand: Gold demand is strong, driven by central bank buying and investor hedging against inflation/policy risks. Antimony demand is inelastic, essential for military munitions and growing clean energy technologies. Overall: Perpetua is positioned at the intersection of two powerful bull markets: precious metals and strategic critical minerals.

**Products**: Gold and Antimony (co-product). Marketing: The "restoration mining" model, which uses mining proceeds to fund environmental cleanup, is a powerful narrative and competitive advantage, garnering significant public and government support. The strategic importance of antimony to U.S. national defense is also a key marketing point.

Department of Defense funding supports studies on producing military-specification antimony trisulfide. Segments: Mining development and future production.

Stibnite Gold Project is located in Idaho, a Tier-1 mining jurisdiction in the United States. The mine plan is designed to maximize early cash flow through a high-grade starter pit, achieving rapid payback.

The project is unique in its design to fund comprehensive environmental restoration of a historic industrial site using mining proceeds.
