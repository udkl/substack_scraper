### **Brief Business Description**

Lectra SA is a French technology company that provides industrialintelligence solutions, including software (CAD/CAM and Product Lifecycle Management - PLM), specialized automated cutting equipment, data analytics, and associated services, primarily for the fashion, automotive, and furniture industries. The company is actively transitioning to a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) model and implementing Industry 4.0 technologies.

### **Core Investment Highlights**

* **Accelerating SaaS Transition:** Rapidly growing Software-as-a-Service revenue, which reached $88.09 million (15% of total) in 2024 and is targeted to exceed $102.37 million in 2025. This shift creates a higher-quality, more predictable recurring revenue stream (72% of total revenue in 2024).
* **Dominant Market Leader:** Commands an estimated 80% global market share in its core segments following the acquisition of Gerber Technology.
* **Industry 4.0 Leadership:** Strong, integrated portfolio of solutions positioning the company to capitalize on digitalization and automation megatrends.
* **Strong Economic Moat:** High customer switching costs and significant barriers to entry for competitors.
* **Robust Financial Profile & Shareholder Focus:** Resilient performance in challenging macroeconomic conditions, robust free cash flow generation ($95.546 million in FY2024), and a healthy balance sheet. Increased dividend payout ratio to 50% of net income for FY2024.

### **Investment Thesis & Market Context**

Lectra SA offers a compelling investment opportunity as the established leader in providing Industry 4.0 solutions for the fashion, automotive, and furniture sectors. The company's strategic pivot to a SaaS model, successful integration of key acquisitions like Gerber Technology and Launchmetrics, and its robust product portfolio create a strong, durable competitive advantage. Coupled with its resilient financials and improving governance, Lectra is well-positioned to capitalize on the ongoing digitalization and automation megatrends, especially as global trade uncertainties drive demand for increased efficiency and supply chain agility.

#### **Market Context: Industry 4.0 and Global Trade**

* **Digitalization & Automation Megatrend:** Industries are increasingly adopting automation and digitalization to improve efficiency, sustainability, and profitability.
* **Global Trade Uncertainties:** Geopolitical tensions and the threat of tariffs are forcing manufacturers to reconfigure supply chains, find new efficiencies, and diversify production locations, directly increasing demand for Lectra's solutions.
* **SaaS Market Growth:** The shift to cloud-based Software-as-a-Service models is creating predictable, high-quality recurring revenue streams and fostering deeper customer engagement.
* **Market Growth:** The overall markets Lectra serves are experiencing healthy growth, including PLM in Fashion (projected CAGR of 12.5%), Fabric Cutting Machines (4.63%), and Automotive Software (7.8%).

#### **Lectra's Strategic Positioning**

* **Clear Market Leader:** Commands over 80% of the global market share in its core segments after the Gerber acquisition.
* **Pioneering Status:** Long history (founded 1973, IPO 1987) as a technology enabler for its industries.
* **Key Differentiator – Integrated Ecosystem & SaaS Pivot:** Offers a comprehensive suite of hardware and software solutions that integrate seamlessly, with all new software now available as SaaS. This creates a powerful "flywheel effect" and enhances customer stickiness.
* **Tariff Resilience:** Lectra's solutions (e.g., Retviews for market intelligence, Valia for agile manufacturing, Kubix Link for PLM, TextileGenesis for traceability) enable manufacturers to adapt to tariffs by improving efficiency, agility, and supply chain visibility.

#### **Competitive Advantages, Moat & Business Model**

* **Brand & Trust:** Cultivated long-standing relationships with major global brands over decades.
* **High Switching Costs:** Lectra's solutions are deeply embedded into customer workflows, requiring substantial training and complex data migration, making it expensive and disruptive for customers to switch providers. This is evidenced by a 96% "security ratio" where recurring revenue gross margin covers fixed overheads.
* **Market Concentration (Lectra/Gerber Dominance):** With an estimated 80% global market share post-Gerber acquisition, customers have relatively few viable alternatives for comprehensive, integrated solutions.
* **SaaS Model Stickiness:** Cloud-based delivery and continuous updates make SaaS offerings inherently "stickier" than traditional software, fostering ongoing customer engagement and driving continuous product improvement.
* **Proactive Supplier Risk Management:** Diligent approach to managing supplier risks, particularly for critical components like semiconductors, enhances reliability and can lead to market share gains.

#### **Integrated Business Model: Hardware, Software & Services**

Lectra operates as a comprehensive industrial intelligence provider:

* **Hardware (Cutting Solutions):** Advanced automated cutting equipment like Vector, Gerber Paragon, and Versalis.
* **Software (CAD/CAM/PLM):** Industry-standard solutions like Modaris, Gerber AccuMark, and Kubix Link (PLM).
* **SaaS Offerings:** Cloud-native solutions for PLM (Kubix Link), market intelligence (Retviews), brand performance (Launchmetrics), traceability (TextileGenesis), cutting room optimization (Valia), and e-commerce (Neteven).
* **Associated Services:** Data analytics and support services.

#### **Industry Attractiveness & Competition (Brief Porter's Summary)**

The industries Lectra serves (fashion, automotive, furniture) are undergoing significant transformation due to Industry 4.0 and global trade dynamics, creating a robust growth outlook. Lectra holds a strong competitive position due to its dominant market share and high barriers to entry.

* **Intensity of Rivalry:** Moderate post-Gerber acquisition, as the merged entity holds an estimated 80% market share globally, with the remaining market fragmented.
* **Threat of New Entrants:** Moderate to Low, due to high capital costs, significant R&D investment, established customer relationships, and high switching costs.
* **Bargaining Power of Suppliers:** Moderate, Lectra manages dependencies on components (e.g., semiconductors) through strategic mitigation.
* **Bargaining Power of Buyers (Customers):** Moderate to Low, limited by high switching costs and Lectra's market dominance.
* **Threat of Substitution:** Low to Moderate, as manual processes are increasingly uncompetitive, and in-house development of comparable sophisticated solutions is prohibitively expensive.

#### **Financial Performance Summary & Capital Allocation**

* **Consistent Growth:** Revenues grew by 10.32% year-over-year to $569.787 million in 2024, including $46.89 million from Launchmetrics. SaaS revenue surged by 150% in 2024.
* **Improving Profitability:** Overall gross profit margin improved from 66.61% in FY22 to 71.56% in FY24. EBITDA margin improved to 17.3% in 2024.
* **Strong Balance Sheet:** Described as "particularly robust" with manageable net financial debt of $23.43 million by December 31, 2024.
* **Robust Free Cash Flow (FCF):** Generated an impressive $95.546 million for the full year 2024. The company also reported a free cash flow conversion rate exceeding 120% in 2022.

#### **Capital Allocation & Shareholder Returns**

Lectra follows a balanced approach, prioritizing investment in innovation (R&D expenditure at 12.8% of revenues in 2024) and strategic acquisitions. The company demonstrates a commitment to returning capital to shareholders through dividends, increasing the payout ratio to 50% of net income for FY2024 due to strong FCF generation. Proposed dividend for FY2024 is $0.43 per share, an 10.26% increase year-over-year.

### **Valuation Glimpse**

* **P/E Ratio (Forward):** Approximately 17.66x.
* **PEG Ratio (Forward):** A compelling 0.66, based on a forward P/E of 17.66x and forecasted EPS growth of 36.46%. A PEG ratio below 1.0 often indicates potential undervaluation.
* **Analyst Outlook:** Generally "Buy," with target prices suggesting significant upside (e.g., $34.31, implying 27% upside, we expect it to go higher than 40%).

### **Key Drivers, Risks & Conclusion**

* Continued acceleration of the SaaS transition and adoption of new cloud solutions.
* Successful realization of synergies and cross-selling opportunities from strategic acquisitions like Gerber and Launchmetrics.
* Increased demand for Industry 4.0 solutions as manufacturers seek efficiency and supply chain resilience amidst global trade uncertainties.
* Leveraging its expanding network of SaaS platforms for data insights and future revenue streams.

#### **Key Risks Summary**

* **Macroeconomic Headwinds & Customer Investment Cycles:** Sensitivity to overall economic conditions and consumer sentiment can delay customer investments.
* **Execution on M&A Integration:** Challenges in integrating multiple acquired technologies could lead to disruptions.
* **Competitive Pressures:** While market leader, faces competition from niche players and diversified software companies.
* **Supply Chain Vulnerabilities:** Dependence on electronic components like semiconductors.
* **Geographic Concentration of Performance:** Growth can be reliant on specific regions (e.g., Asian automotive in Q1 2024).
* **Recent Share Price Underperformance:** Classified as a "Falling Star" by Stockopedia, reflecting recent negative price momentum.

### **Management Snapshot**

* **Founder-Led:** Led by Daniel Harari, Chairman and CEO, since the early 1990s, providing over three decades of consistent leadership. Mr. Harari holds a significant personal stake of 12.7%.
* **Proactive Succession Planning:** An "ad hoc Committee" is tasked with overseeing the CEO succession plan.
* **Improved Governance:** Elimination of double voting rights effective April 2025 aligns with best corporate governance practices.
* **Experienced Executive Committee:** Includes Olivier du Chesnay (CFO since 2017) and Michael Jaïs (CEO & Co-founder of Launchmetrics).

Lectra SA (LSS on Euronext Paris) represents a high-quality, market-leading company in the transforming industrial technology sector. Its decisive SaaS pivot, strategic acquisitions, and dominant market position create a strong and durable economic moat. Supported by consistent financial performance, robust free cash flow generation, and a shareholder-friendly capital allocation policy, Lectra appears well-positioned for sustained growth. While trading at a premium to some traditional industrial peers, its valuation seems justified by its superior growth prospects and market position, especially given the low forward PEG ratio. Lectra warrants serious consideration for investors seeking exposure to the compelling Industry 4.0 transformation and the increasing demand for operational resilience in a fragmented global trade environment.
