# **Recent Developments: Tailwinds**

## **1. Canadian Carbon Policy Has Shifted**

The regulatory outlook has improved materially since mid-2025.

Prime Minister Mark Carney eliminated the consumer carbon tax in April 2025. The oil and gas emissions cap, which would have imposed hard limits on production, appears unlikely to proceed. Alberta has frozen its industrial carbon price at C$95/tonne through 2026.

The previous concern about carbon tax escalation to C$170/tonne by 2030 is now dead policy. Operating cost risk from carbon regulation has diminished significantly. This is an unambiguous tailwind.

## **2. TMX Is Open**

The Trans Mountain Expansion completed in May 2024, tripling pipeline capacity to Canada’s Pacific Coast from 300,000 to 890,000 barrels per day.

This provides:

* Access to Asian markets for the first time
* Structural narrowing of WCS-WTI differentials

# **Risks: What Could Go Wrong?**

Intellectual honesty requires identifying the scenarios where this investment fails.

## **Thesis Killer #1: Sustained Low Oil Prices**

**The scenario:** OPEC+ abandons production discipline. US shale extends Tier-1 inventory through technology. Demand peaks earlier than expected. Global recession. Oil settles at $45-55 for years.

**Impact on CNQ:** EBITDA collapses from C$16B to C$7-9B. Free cash flow evaporates after sustaining capex. Dividend becomes unsustainable. Stock re-rates to C$25-30/share.

**Probability: 10%.** This requires multiple negative scenarios to align simultaneously.

**Mitigant:** CNQ’s breakeven is ~$40 WTI, the lowest among peers. They survive when others fail. But “surviving” is not “thriving.”

## **Thesis Killer #2: The Supply Thesis Is Wrong**

**The contrarian view:** Technology keeps extending Tier-1 acreage. OPEC+ releases spare capacity at $90, capping the upside. China’s oil demand falls faster than expected. The EIA is right.

**Impact if thesis wrong:** Oil trades sideways ($65-75) for years. CNQ is fine, but no re-rating occurs. You earn the 5% dividend and nothing else. Opportunity cost versus other investments.

**Probability of sideways oil: 25%.**

## **Thesis Killer #3: Terminal Value Impairment**

**The long-term scenario:** IEA Net Zero materializes. Global oil demand peaks by 2030 and declines thereafter. CNQ’s 33-year reserve life becomes stranded asset liability.

**Probability over 10+ year horizon: 20-30%.**

**Mitigant:** Already partially priced in. CNQ trades at a significant discount to historical multiples. The 5% dividend compensates for terminal value uncertainty. You are not paying for reserves beyond 2040.
