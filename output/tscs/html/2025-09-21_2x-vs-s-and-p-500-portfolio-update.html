Portfolio Updates

Catalysts Materializing: From Potential to Proof

Perpetua Resources ($PPTA.TSE): A Generational De-Risking Event

Our position in Perpetua Resources, now up 32.14%, has been predicated on the market mispricing the immense strategic value of its Stibnite Gold Project. While the financing package announced earlier this year was transformative, a recent development has fundamentally and permanently altered the risk profile of this asset.

On September 8, 2025, Perpetua announced it had received a Preliminary Project Letter and a non-binding indicative financing term sheet from the Export-Import Bank of the United States ("EXIM") for potential debt financing of up to $2 billion.

EXIM is not a commercial bank; it is an official export credit agency of the U.S. Federal Government.

This is far more than a loan commitment; it is a powerful, implicit endorsement of the Stibnite project's critical importance to the United States. The project is set to become the only domestic mined source of antimony, a material vital for national defense.

EXIM's involvement, with a potential final board consideration by the spring of 2026, effectively provides a sovereign stamp of approval, dramatically reducing the political and permitting risk that has historically weighed on the stock.

This development creates a cascade of value.

The EXIM term sheet provides Perpetua with immense leverage in all other financing discussions. The "potential royalty or stream arrangement" mentioned in the company's press release can now be negotiated from a position of profound strength.

Potential partners are now aware that the project's primary debt is backstopped by a sovereign entity, making any other slice of the capital structure significantly safer. This is how a generational asset is de-risked, and we are witnessing the market begin to re-price the equity accordingly.

Anicom Holdings ($8715.TYO): The Sound of Silence

Our top performer, Anicom Holdings, continues its steady ascent, now up 32.96% since inception. Since our last update, the news flow from the company has been notably quiet. This silence is, in fact, the thesis playing out perfectly.

Anicom is the quintessential "quiet compounder."

It is the dominant pet insurer in Japan, a market with structural tailwinds, and it is executing its business plan flawlessly, far from the glare of the financial media. The last significant data point was its first-quarter earnings report on August 7, which beat both revenue and EPS estimates .

The "smart money" we noted last month (Dalton Investments and Hikari Telecommunication) did not invest for a quick, news-driven catalyst. They, like us, invested in the steady, predictable, and profitable growth of a superior business. The stock's strong performance reflects the market slowly awakening to the fundamental value we and other sophisticated investors have already identified.

Anicom's journey is a crucial lesson: value can be realized not just through a single "big bang" event like a takeover, but through the relentless, incremental progress of a dominant niche business.

Alamos Gold ($AGI.TSE): Earning a Premium Valuation

Alamos Gold, up 30.35%, continues to prove that it is far more than a simple proxy for the price of gold.

On August 28, the company declared its regular quarterly dividend, reinforcing its commitment to shareholder returns and its status as a robust cash-generating machine.

More significantly, on September 9, Alamos was recognized by the Toronto Stock Exchange with inclusion in the prestigious TSX30 list for the second consecutive year; a third-party validation of its superior stock performance and operational excellence.

This type of recognition is critical, as it attracts new classes of institutional investors who screen for quality and operational leadership.

Each of these data points, the strong Q2 results and technical report we discussed last month, the consistent dividend and now the TSX30 inclusion, is another brick in the wall of credibility that Alamos is building.

This forces the market to value the company not on the daily fluctuations of a commodity, but on the tangible, multi-billion-dollar net present value of its Island Gold asset and its proven ability to return capital. The company is systematically proving it deserves a premium valuation relative to its peers, and we are benefiting as that re-rating occurs.

Steady Execution and Thesis Reaffirmation

AcadeMedia ($ACAD.STO): The Predictable Compounder

On August 29, our Swedish education provider published its full year-end report, confirming the strong preliminary figures we had anticipated. The final results showed net sales growth of 5.4% and an adjusted operating profit increase of over 12%.

These figures represent a model of boring excellence, which is precisely why we own the stock. The report confirmed the business's role as a defensive, non-correlated compounder in our portfolio.

Furthermore, the CEO's commentary highlighted that international operations, a key vector of our growth thesis, now represent more than 40% of total sales. AcadeMedia is performing its job perfectly: providing stable, predictable growth that helps insulate our portfolio from broader macroeconomic volatility.

Vetoquinol ($VETO.EURONEXT): Underlying Quality Shines Through

In our last letter, we noted that the market was awaiting Vetoquinol's first-half results to confirm that temporary US supplier issues were resolved. The report, released on September 11, provided that confirmation and more.

The company posted sales of €258 million, a strong EBITDA of €53 million (representing a robust 20.4% margin), and net income of €25 million. Critically, sales of high-margin "Essentials" products grew 4.2% at constant exchange rates, and the report noted a rebound in the US during the second quarter. This was a clear validation of our thesis. While headline sales were relatively flat, the profitability and quality of those sales improved significantly; a crucial distinction that short-term market participants often miss. The underlying quality and pricing power of this family-controlled, cash-rich business are shining through.

Weatherford International ($WFRD.NASDAQ): The Street Plays Catch-Up

Since its Q2 earnings report, Weatherford has continued to perform well. The most notable development has been further external validation from the investment community.

On August 20, Melius Research initiated coverage on the company. This is a clear sign that sell-side analysts are being forced to acknowledge the powerful operational turnaround that we identified months ago.

Our thesis was that the company's internal execution was far stronger than its languishing stock price suggested. The Q2 earnings were the proof. Now, the rest of the Street is playing catch-up, providing a steady tailwind of new buyers as the story becomes more widely known and understood.

Conviction Amidst Consolidation

Lectra ($LSS.EURONEXT): The Patience Test

Lectra remains our most frustrating holding, currently down 2.05% since inception. The stock has drifted sideways since management withdrew its 2025 guidance in late July.

The market has placed the company in the "penalty box" for this lack of short-term visibility. This situation, however, presents a perfect real-time lesson in our investment philosophy. Our thesis is not based on Lectra hitting its 2025 guidance; it is based on its multi-year strategic transition to a higher-margin, more predictable Software as a Service (SaaS) business model.

The recent news flow has been operational, focused on new product insights and customer wins rather than financially negative. The absence of fundamental deterioration is, in this context, a positive. It allows the company to execute its complex transition away from the market's impatient quarterly gaze. This is our new "frustrating" holding, a fundamentally sound business being punished for sentiment, creating an opportunity for patient, long-term investors.

Karnov Group ($KAR.STO) & Asseco Poland ($ACP.WAR): Attractive Prices for Quality Businesses

Our two new European niche leaders are both slightly down since we initiated our positions, presenting a profound disconnect between price and value.

On August 21, Karnov Group released strong Q2 results, showing accelerating organic growth of 4.6% and an improved adjusted EBITA margin of 22.8%. This growth was explicitly linked to the successful adoption of its new AI tools, providing direct proof that our "digital toll road" thesis is not just intact, but strengthening.

Similarly, Asseco Poland's H1 results showed robust 8% year-over-year sales growth and even faster 14% non-IFRS operating profit growth, confirming its status as a disciplined compounder.

Here we have two companies whose stocks have drifted lower while their underlying businesses have reported improving fundamentals. This is the exact type of inefficiency our strategy is designed to exploit. The market's short-term indifference to this positive operational news is a gift, allowing us to own these high-quality, niche-dominant businesses at an even more attractive valuation than when we first invested.

Whitbread PLC ($WTB.LSE): Management's Vote of Confidence

Our UK hospitality leader is holding steady. The most significant news since our entry has been management's consistent execution of its share buy-back program. A share buyback is one of the most powerful and tangible signals management can send.

It is a capital-backed statement that they believe their own stock is the best use of corporate funds, in other words, that it is undervalued. This action directly aligns their interests with ours. It simultaneously reduces the number of shares outstanding, increasing our per-share ownership of the business, and provides a source of steady demand for the stock. This quiet, shareholder-friendly capital allocation is precisely what we expect from a resilient, well-managed compounder.

A New Opportunity in UK Wholesale

With the Just Group thesis now fully realized, we have redeployed a portion of that capital into a new opportunity that fits our criteria: a niche leader with a long runway for growth that we believe is underappreciated by the market.

Introducing Kitwave Group Plc ($KITW.LSE)

Our newest holding is Kitwave Group, a UK-based "delivered wholesale" business. The company operates a network of 37 depots, supplying a diverse range of approximately 44,000 products, from confectionery and soft drinks to frozen foods and alcohol, to a customer base of roughly 46,000 independent businesses, including convenience retailers, vending operators, and foodservice providers.

The investment thesis is centered on Kitwave's role as a proven, disciplined consolidator in the highly fragmented UK wholesale market. The company's core strategy is to execute "roll-up" acquisitions, buying smaller, independent wholesalers and integrating them into its larger, more efficient network. Its competitive advantage lies in serving the long tail of the market, customers whose order sizes are too small for larger competitors to service profitably, with minimum order values as low as £100.

We see an opportunity to partner with a management team that has a long and successful track record of value-accretive acquisitions, operating in a defensive industry. This is a classic small-cap compounder, hiding in plain sight.
