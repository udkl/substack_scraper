Investment Thesis

Strengths

Karnov operates a high-quality, subscription-led business model characterized by exceptional customer retention, strong pricing power, and formidable barriers to entry. In its core Nordic markets (Region North), the company commands dominant market positions, consistently delivering impressive operating margins exceeding 40%. This regional strength is a proof to the mission-critical nature of its services and the deep integration into its customers' workflows.

The recent push of Karnov’s AI tools shows a significant accelerant for revenue growth. These tools are priced at a substantial 30% premium to standard licenses, reflecting the clear return on investment (ROI) legal professionals are experiencing through improved efficiency. The cost of a Karnov AI license is a fraction of a junior associate’s salary, making the value proposition undeniable. This tangible monetization of AI makes Karnov one of the most compelling AI beneficiaries observed in public markets today.

The business model inherently generates strong, predictable cash flows, primarily driven by annual online subscriptions that are often paid in advance. This provides financial resilience and flexibility. Region North continues to be a powerhouse, demonstrating strong organic growth of 7.7% in Q1 2025, fueled by volume, pricing adjustments, and AI uplifts. Furthermore, the strategic acquisitions, particularly in Southern Europe, are now yielding material synergies, which are visibly contributing to margin expansion across the group, with adjusted EBITA margins improving from 19.7% in 2023 to 22.4% in 2024 and further to 26% in Q1 2025. This upward trajectory in profitability is a direct result of diligent integration efforts.

At the core of Karnov's strength lies its authored content, developed by over 7,000 renowned legal experts. This vast, high-quality content base is the indispensable foundation for reliable and accurate AI solutions. Given that Karnov's services are mission-critical to its customers, yet represent a low proportion of their overall operating costs, the company enjoys considerable pricing power and customer stickiness.

Weaknesses

Despite its many strengths Karnov has challenges, particularly concerning its recent acquisitions in Southern Europe (Region South). These integrations initially introduced lower margins and slower growth rates compared to the established Nordic operations. For instance, Region South experienced a -1.7% organic growth in Q1 2025, largely due to a decline in offline sales in Spain. This segment's larger scale of offline business presents a structural headwind as the market shifts towards digital. Furthermore, product innovation in these newly acquired markets had lagged during the initial integration phase. The company also reported a negative net income in 2024, primarily due to higher financial expenses and significant items affecting comparability related to post-merger integration and restructuring costs.

Opportunities

The path forward for Karnov has significant opportunities. The company is actively accelerating product innovation in Southern Europe over the next 12 months, aiming to revitalize growth in these markets. A key driver will be the continued monetization and expansion of its AI offerings, including the rollout of new features and specialized solutions for new segments, such as case workers in municipalities.

The most impactful opportunity lies in material margin expansion driven by the realization of synergies. Karnov is on track to lift group-wide adjusted EBITA margins materially from 22% to over 25% in the medium term and exceeding 30% in the long term. The company has already achieved EUR 17 million in annual run-rate synergies by Q1 2025, nearing its EUR 20 million target by the end of 2026. This operational leverage will significantly boost profitability.

Beyond organic growth, Karnov is strategically positioned for geographical expansion and vertical growth through selective acquisitions. The company's ability to leverage its strong local content and European innovation capabilities to create scalable AI products across diverse markets represents a powerful growth vector.

Threats

While the outlook is bullish, potential headwinds warrant consideration. Competition from freely available public information sources, though currently more impactful on smaller law firms, could evolve to challenge larger segments. New market entrants or disruptive business models, while facing high barriers, could emerge. We believe there will be a warning signal before the disruption fully materializes. The ongoing integration of acquisitions has the potential for loss of key individuals or customer churn if not managed effectively. Furthermore, the company's reliance on key personnel and its extensive network of authors and experts highlights the importance of talent retention. Lastly, as a digital services provider, cybersecurity risks remain a constant concern, necessitating continuous vigilance and investment in robust protection measures.

Porter Competitive Advantage

Karnov Group operates within an industry structure that inherently favors established players. This gives it a strong moat.

Porter and Competitive Industry Position

  • Suppliers: 8/10
    Karnov's value proposition is its extensive proprietary content, authored by over 7,000 legal experts. This reliance on specialized intellectual capital means that while content creators are crucial, Karnov's long-standing relationships and the sheer volume of its proprietary database significantly reduce the bargaining power of individual content suppliers. The company effectively controls its primary input, ensuring consistent quality and differentiation.

  • Customers: 9/10
    Karnov's services are mission-critical for legal professionals. The cost of these services represents a low proportion of the customers' overall operating expenses and they deliver immense value through improved efficiency and certainty in complex legal workflows. This creates high switching costs, as transitioning to a new provider would entail significant disruption, retraining, and potential loss of historical data integration. Consequently, customer retention rates are exceptionally high: approximately 97% in Region North and 90% in Region South. This strong stickiness translates directly into low customer bargaining power.

  • Barriers to Entry and Exit: 9/10
    The legal information market is characterized by substantial barriers to entry. Building a high-quality legal database requires enormous amounts of content accumulation, deep domain expertise, and a network of authoritative legal experts. The need for locally tailored content in each civil law jurisdiction further complicates market entry for new players. Established customer relationships, built on trust and reliability over decades, are also difficult to replicate. These factors collectively create a significant moat, protecting Karnov from new entrants.

  • Threat of Substitution: 7/10 The threat is low, as substitutes like free public data and generic AI tools lack the reliability of Karnov’s proprietary, expert-authored content, which is mission-critical for clients. For legal professionals, the high risk of inaccuracy from using a substitute far outweighs the minimal cost savings. Crucially, Karnov has neutralized the generic AI threat by developing its own premium AI assistant, KAILA, turning a potential disruption into a key competitive advantage.

  • Competition: 7/10
    While the Nordic markets are highly consolidated, Southern Europe presents more competition. Competition from large global players such as Thomson Reuters, Wolters Kluwer, and RELX Group, as well as regional private companies like Lefebvre Sarrut and VLex is seen. However, Karnov is actively strengthening its foothold in Southern Europe through strategic integrations and product enhancements. The ongoing industry trend towards consolidation further benefits established players with strong balance sheets and integration capabilities.

  • Overall Comment: 40/50
    Karnov Group operates within an exceptionally attractive industry structure. The combination of strong control over its unique content, high customer stickiness, and substantial barriers to entry provides a robust foundation for sustained profitability and market leadership. The competitive intensity, while present, is mitigated by the specialized nature of the market and Karnov's entrenched positions.

Key Competitors

Karnov's competitive landscape includes both large, diversified global players and more regionally focused entities. Key listed competitors with similar operational models include Thomson Reuters, Wolters Kluwer, RELX Group, and Dye & Durham. Additionally, private companies such as Lefebvre Sarrut, VLex, and Tirant lo blanch represent regional competitive forces.

Competitive Analysis

Karnov's historical depth of content and long-standing customer relationships, creates a powerful network effect. Furthermore, Karnov's proactive and tailored integration of AI into its platforms, such as the KAILA legal research assistant, is a significant differentiator. The ability to deliver AI solutions that understand and replicate local legal methods and provide precise, source-referenced answers gives Karnov a distinct edge over more generic AI tools. The 30% premium on AI licenses demonstrates the market's recognition of this superior value.

Sustainable Competitive Advantage

  • Proprietary Content: The vast, quality-assured, and locally tailored legal content database is an intangible asset that is virtually impossible for competitors to replicate quickly or cost-effectively.

  • High Switching Costs: As discussed, the integration of Karnov's services into daily legal workflows creates significant friction for customers considering alternatives.

  • Network Effects: The extensive network of 7,000+ authors and experts who contribute to and enrich Karnov's content creates a virtuous cycle, attracting more users and further solidifying the platform's authority.

  • First-Mover/Early-Adopter Advantage in AI Integration: By being at the forefront of integrating AI into its specialized legal information services, Karnov is setting the standard and capturing market share, further enhancing its competitive position.

Operational Overview

Products, Geographies, Segments, Market Share, Capacity

Karnov offers a comprehensive suite of products and services created to the needs of legal and other professionals. These include:

  • Legal Research: Digital platforms providing extensive legal materials, statutes, case law, and expert commentaries, such as JUNO and Karnov Online in the North, and Aranzadi Digital and Lamyline in the South.

  • Process and Compliance Tools: Solutions like DIBkunnskap and Notisum in Region North, and Aranzadi Fusion and Echoline in Region South, designed to enhance workflow efficiency and ensure regulatory compliance.

  • Legal Training: Both digital and physical courses for continuing education and specialization, offered through platforms like NJ E-courses and Lamy Formation.

  • Printed Matter: Traditional books and journals, though this segment is seeing declining demand as the shift to digital accelerates.

Geographically, Karnov operates in two primary regions:

  • Region North: Encompassing Denmark, Sweden, and Norway, this market is more homogeneous and highly consolidated, with Karnov holding dominant market leadership.

  • Region South: Covering France, Spain, and Portugal, this market is more diverse and fragmented, where Karnov is actively expanding its market presence and integrating recent acquisitions.

The company serves a diverse range of customer segments:

  • Practitioners: Including individual legal practitioners, law firms, and accounting/auditing firms.

  • Corporate: In-house legal departments and corporate entities requiring legal compliance and advisory services.

  • Public Sector: Government agencies, courts, universities, and municipalities.

Karnov's market share is strong, holding overall market leadership in Denmark, Sweden, and Spain, and a leadership position in labor law in France. The company boasts an impressive capacity, serving over 400,000 active users across its platforms in six countries, supported by approximately 1,200 employees and a vast network of over 7,000 contributing legal experts.

Key operational initiatives are driving the company's change:

  • AI Development: The launch and enhancement of AI-driven solutions like KAILA in Region North, which is being adopted by major law firms and the public sector, is a central focus. These AI tools are designed to deliver significant efficiency gains and are monetized at a premium, accelerating revenue growth.

  • Synergy Realization: A critical operational lever is the aggressive pursuit of synergies from both the Region South integration and a group-wide Acceleration Initiative. The company targets EUR 20 million in total annual run-rate cost synergies by the end of 2026, with EUR 17 million already achieved by Q1 2025 on a run-rate basis. This is translating directly into margin improvement.

  • Region South Turnaround: Operational focus in Region South includes a successful top-line turnaround in France, driven by online growth and new product launches, and an action plan to address weak offline sales in Spain. The launch of the Infinita product in Spain, integrating AI and merged content, is a key step in this region's revitalization.

  • Schultz Integration: The integration of the carved-out Schultz legal information business in Denmark is progressing as planned, expanding Karnov's customer base in the Danish municipality market and strengthening its content portfolio for generative AI applications.

Industry Overview

Sector description, market structure, trends

The sector provides essential knowledge and workflow solutions to professionals in law, tax, accounting, environmental, and health protection. It is a mature market, but it is advancing technologically.

The market structure exhibits distinct characteristics:

  • Non-Cyclical Demand: Karnov's customer base is highly non-cyclical and financially resilient, ensuring stable demand regardless of broader economic fluctuations. This provides a strong defensive quality to the business.

  • High Customer Retention: The market is defined by long-standing customer relationships, with very low churn rates (97% in Region North, 90% in Region South) due to the business-critical nature of the services and the high switching costs involved.

  • Low Share of Operating Costs: Despite being indispensable, Karnov's services represent a small fraction of its customers' overall operating expenses, enabling strong pricing power.

  • Consolidation: The Nordic market is highly consolidated with fewer players, while Southern and Central European markets are more fragmented, presenting opportunities for further consolidation.

Several trends as tailwinds for Karnov:

  • Increasing Regulatory Complexity: Driven by globalization, increased focus on sustainability (ESG), and new areas of legislation (e.g., IT regulation, AI Act), the sheer volume and complexity of legal and regulatory information are continuously expanding. This directly increases the need for sophisticated information tools and expert interpretations.

  • Digitalization and Automation: The legal profession is undergoing a significant shift towards digital tools and automation to enhance efficiency and streamline workflows. This trend is accelerating, with firms seeking solutions to manage vast amounts of information and automate routine tasks.

  • AI Transformation: Artificial Intelligence is rapidly becoming a transformative force in legal tech. AI solutions are not only improving legal interpretation and application but also becoming an area of increasing regulation themselves. Karnov's strategic focus on AI, leveraging its proprietary content to deliver precise and reliable answers, positions it at the forefront of this trend.

  • Shift to Online Services: There is a clear and accelerating migration from traditional offline information sources (like printed books) to digital online platforms. Karnov is well-aligned with this trend, with online sales representing the vast majority of its revenue and growing strongly.

Revenue Trends & Drivers, Margins, and Profitability

Revenue trends

Overall, Karnov has exhibited solid revenue growth. Net sales increased by 4.8% to SEK 2,593 million in 2024, compared to SEK 2,475 million in 2023. This growth continued into Q1 2025, with net sales rising by 7% to SEK 673 million from SEK 632 million in Q1 2024.

Revenue mix reveals a significant and positive trend:

  • Online Sales: This segment is the primary growth engine. In Region North, online sales surged by 19% in Q1 2025 compared to the previous year, now accounting for 88% of net sales in that region. Region South also saw online sales increase by 4% in Q1 2025, representing approximately 80% of its net sales. This strong performance in online channels aligns with the broader industry shift towards digital solutions.

  • Offline Sales: Conversely, offline sales have experienced a decline, particularly in Spain, which contributed to the overall -1.7% organic growth in Region South in Q1 2025. This is a recognized trend across the industry, and Karnov is actively managing this transition.

  • Subscription-based Revenue: A key characteristic of Karnov's revenue model is its high proportion of subscription-based sales, which constituted 88% of total sales in Q1 2025. These subscriptions are typically paid in advance, ensuring a strong and predictable cash flow profile for the company.

The overarching trend is a successful pivot towards digital, subscription-based services, strengthened by strategic acquisitions and innovative product development. The growth in Region North remains consistently strong, while Region South is showing signs of stabilization and targeted growth in online segments, despite the headwinds from offline sales.

Volume growth is a significant driver, especially in Region North, where increased demand for legal information solutions and customer upgrades to AI packages are expanding the customer base. The acquisition of the carved-out Schultz business in Denmark, for example, directly expanded Karnov's customer base in the Danish municipality market.

Product/Services

The shift in product mix towards higher-value, AI-integrated online solutions is a crucial revenue driver. Karnov's AI assistant, priced at a 30% premium, is being adopted across all customer segments, leading to increased usage and accelerating revenue recognition as subscription revenues are recognized over the contract period. This premium offering enhances the overall average revenue per user.

Pricing

Karnov demonstrates strong pricing power, particularly with its AI-enhanced offerings. This pricing power is a testament to the mission-critical nature of its services and the high ROI they provide to legal professionals.

Margins and ROCE analysis

Adjusted EBITA Margin: The group adjusted EBITA margin improved significantly from 19.7% in 2023 to 22.4% in 2024. This positive trend accelerated into Q1 2025, reaching 26%, a notable improvement of over 3 percentage points from Q1 2024. This puts Karnov well on track towards its medium-term financial target of ≥25% and long-term target of ≥30% adjusted EBITA margin.

Regional Margins:

  • Region North: Continues to exhibit exceptional profitability, with an adjusted EBITA margin of 43.0% in 2024, further improving to 46.6% in Q1 2025. This is driven by strong online sales performance and efficiencies from the Acceleration Initiative.

  • Region South: While starting from a lower base due to integration challenges, the adjusted EBITA margin in Region South improved from 9.3% in 2023 to 11.1% in 2024, and further to 12.2% in Q1 2025. This improvement is primarily due to lower employee expenses as synergies from the integration are realized.

  • Synergy Contribution: The materialization of cost synergies is a direct contributor to margin expansion. In Q1 2025, synergies contributed SEK 41 million (EUR 3.6 million) to profitability. The company has achieved EUR 17 million in annual run-rate synergies by Q1 2025, progressing towards its total target of EUR 20 million by the end of 2026. These synergies stem from both the Region South integration and the group-wide Acceleration Initiative, focusing on product rationalization, process streamlining, and IT infrastructure harmonization.

Cost Structure Summary

Karnov's cost structure is being optimized through strategic initiatives. Personnel expenses are notably decreasing as a result of synergy achievements. While the company is investing in exploratory AI projects for future customer value, these costs are integrated into operating expenses, reflecting a commitment to innovation that is expected to yield future profitability. The focus is on driving operational leverage, where increased revenues translate into disproportionately higher profits due to a more efficient cost base.

Valuation, Reverse Thesis, and Conclusion

Valuation & Margin of Safety

The current valuation of Karnov Group presents a compelling entry point for long-term investors, offering a significant margin of safety. The shares currently trade at a steep forward discount compared to larger, more established peers like Thomson Reuters, both on earnings and sales multiples. This disparity suggests that the market may not yet be fully appreciating the transformative impact of Karnov's AI monetization and the material margin expansion that is becoming increasingly visible.

The forward EV/EBIT for 2025FY, at 22.10 represents a reasonable multiple for a company with a strong competitive moat, high recurring revenues, and significant growth catalysts.

The company's robust free cash flow generation further underpins its valuation. In Q1 2025 alone, Karnov generated SEK 245 million in adjusted free cash flow, a substantial improvement from the previous year. This strong cash generation provides financial flexibility for continued investment in growth and synergies, while also bolstering the balance sheet. The company's leverage has improved to 2.4x, well below its financial target of ≤3.0x, indicating a healthy financial position and capacity for future strategic moves. This combination of a forward discounted valuation, strong projected earnings recovery, and solid cash flow provides a substantial margin of safety for investors.

Reverse Thesis

While the bullish case for Karnov Group is strong, a prudent investor must consider the potential pitfalls. The primary risk lies in the execution of the Region South integration. If the company fails to fully realize the targeted synergies of EUR 20 million by the end of 2026, or if the turnaround in this region, particularly addressing the decline in offline sales in Spain, proves more challenging than anticipated, it could hinder overall margin expansion and growth.

Another critical area is AI adoption. Should the efficiency gains from Karnov's AI assistant not meet customer expectations, or if competitors introduce superior or significantly cheaper AI solutions, the premium pricing and accelerated revenue growth from AI uplifts could be jeopardized. Furthermore, a faster-than-expected decline in traditional offline sales, coupled with insufficient online growth to offset it, could pressure overall revenues and profitability. Regulatory changes that negatively impact the core legal information business, or a failure to attract and retain key talent and the extensive network of expert authors, could also pose headwinds.

Conclusion

Karnov Group represents a compelling long-term investment opportunity for the discerning small-cap strategist. This is a company with a resilient, mission-critical business model, deeply embedded in the workflows of legal professionals across Europe. Its competitive moat, high customer switching costs, and powerful network effects, provides a strong foundation for sustained profitability.

The ongoing AI revolution is a tangible and revenue-generating reality. The successful monetization of its AI assistant, charging a premium for demonstrable efficiency gains, positions Karnov as a true AI beneficiary. Coupled with the aggressive and albeit well-executed synergy realization from its strategic acquisitions, the company is on a clear path to significant margin expansion, targeting adjusted EBITA margins exceeding 25% in the medium term.

Despite its high-quality attributes and clear growth catalysts, Karnov's shares trade at a notable discount to peers (in forward terms), offering an attractive valuation that shows its underlying strength and future potential. The strong free cash flow generation and improving balance sheet provide the financial flexibility needed to continue investing in its strategic priorities. For those willing to look beyond short-term noise and embrace a long-term horizon, Karnov Group is a high-conviction play poised to compound value as it continues to clear the path to justice in an increasingly digital and AI-driven world.
