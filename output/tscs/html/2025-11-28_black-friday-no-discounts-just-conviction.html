You may have noticed we’ve outgrown our name. While our roots are in the overlooked universe of small and medium-sized companies, our methodology applies universally.

We are moving away from “Small Cap Strategist” to simply TSCS.

Our focus isn’t defined by the size of the company, but by the gap between market perception and fundamental reality. TSCS provides institutional-grade research for discerning investors seeking a genuine edge, wherever that edge may be found.
