Business Description

Karnov Group provides essential knowledge and workflow solutions, primarily for legal professionals.

The company makes money through a subscription-led model (88% of sales), offering digital legal research platforms (JUNO, Aranzadi Digital), compliance tools, and legal training.

Its revenue is highly predictable due to annual subscriptions, often paid in advance.

Investment Thesis

Investment Case: A high-quality, mission-critical business with a strong economic moat, trading at a discount. It boasts 40%+ margins and 97% retention in its core market.

The valuation is at a forward discount to peers. Strong free cash flow generation and a healthy balance sheet (leverage at 2.4x) provide downside protection.

What would a short case look like?

The investment thesis could be wrong if:

1) The integration of Region South fails to deliver the targeted cost synergies or revitalize growth.

2) The adoption of AI tools falters, or competitors launch superior solutions, undermining the premium pricing strategy.

3) The decline in traditional offline sales accelerates faster than online growth can compensate.

4) A failure to retain key expert authors erodes the quality of the proprietary content base.

Drivers, Risks, Porters Five

However, one must consider the key risks, such as competition from free public information, the emergence of disruptive new entrants, execution risks in managing post-merger integrations (potential for customer churn or loss of key staff), reliance on its network of 7,000+ authors, and cybersecurity threats.

Competitors & Competitive Advantage

Karnov holds dominant market leadership in its core Nordic markets (Denmark, Sweden) and Spain. It is actively expanding its presence in the more fragmented Southern European markets.

Product differentiation: The key differentiator is its vast, proprietary database of expert-authored, locally-tailored legal content. The proactive integration of its AI assistant, KAILA, which is trained on this unique content, provides a significant edge over generic tools.

The ability to price its AI-driven tools at a 30% premium to standard licenses is clear evidence of strong pricing power, reflecting the high ROI it delivers to customers.

The economic moat is exceptionally wide and durable, built on the foundations of proprietary intellectual property, high customer stickiness, and substantial barriers to entry.

Revenue & Cost

Overall: Solid revenue growth, with net sales up 4.8% in 2024 and 7% in Q1 2025. The business is non-cyclical. Growth is driven by a mix of organic expansion and strategic acquisitions.

Volume: Volume growth is strong, particularly in Region North, driven by new customer acquisition and upgrades to premium AI packages.

Pricing: Strong pricing power is evident, especially with the 30% premium for AI-enhanced services. Pricing is a key lever for revenue growth.

Mix: A positive shift in mix is underway, with high-growth online subscription sales (now 88% of sales in Region North) rapidly replacing declining offline sales.

Cost & Margin

The cost structure is being actively optimized through synergy programs, creating significant operating leverage. Adjusted EBITA margins are on a clear upward trajectory, improving from 19.7% in 2023 to 22.4% in 2024, and reaching 26% in Q1 2025. The company is on track for its target of >30% margin in the long term.

The primary opex is personnel expenses, which are decreasing as synergies are realized. Investments in AI are integrated into operating expenses. Operating leverage is high as revenue growth outpaces cost growth.

Items

The company reported significant items affecting comparability related to post-merger integration and restructuring costs, which led to a net loss in 2024.

Allocation of Capital

The capital allocation framework is disciplined and prioritizes reinvestment for growth over shareholder payouts. The primary uses of cash are:

  1. Strategic M&A,

  2. Investments in synergy realization, and

  3. AI development.

The board's decision to propose no dividend for 2025 underscores this focus on compounding long-term value.

Balance Sheet

The balance sheet is healthy. Leverage has improved to 2.4x net debt/EBITDA, which is comfortably below the company's financial target of ≤3.0x. This provides financial flexibility for future strategic initiatives.

Cash flow

Cash flow generation is strong and predictable. This is driven by the high proportion (88%) of subscription-based revenue, which is typically paid in advance. The company generated SEK 245 million in adjusted free cash flow in Q1 2025 alone.

Valuation

The company trades at a significant forward discount to its larger peers on both earnings and sales multiples. The 2025FYE EV/EBIT of 22.1x is reasonable for a high-quality business with strong growth catalysts. The combination of a discounted valuation, strong FCF, and visible margin expansion provides a substantial margin of safety.

Management

CEO: Pontus Bodelsson (since 2021) has a Master of Laws and extensive leadership experience in media groups, fitting for an information services company.

CFO: Magnus Hansson (since 2023) has a background as CFO of another tech company, bringing crucial financial acumen for managing growth and integration.

The broader management team has deep industry expertise in law, technology, and strategic development, with several long-tenured executives providing institutional knowledge.

Allocation, Industry, and Operational History

The company has a history of growth through strategic M&A, with recent acquisitions including the Schultz legal information business in Denmark and several smaller tech firms in 2024. A key historical event is the major acquisition in Southern Europe which is now the focus of synergy realization.

The legal information services sector is a mature, non-cyclical market characterized by high customer retention due to mission-critical services and high switching costs. Key trends driving growth are increasing regulatory complexity, the ongoing digitalization of the legal profession, and the transformative impact of AI.

Karnov provides a suite of digital and offline legal information products to practitioners, corporations, and the public sector. Operations are divided into Region North (high-margin, consolidated) and Region South (lower-margin, fragmented, turnaround focus). Key initiatives include AI platform development (KAILA), realizing EUR 20m in cost synergies, and revitalizing growth in Southern Europe.
