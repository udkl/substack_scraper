#### **Perpetua Resources ($PPTA.TSE): The Sovereign Backstop**

Our position in Perpetua Resources, now up an incredible **58.93%** since inception, has been predicated on the market mispricing the immense strategic value of its Stibnite Gold Project. In September, two developments transformed this thesis from potential to proof. First, on September 8th, Perpetua announced it had received a Preliminary Project Letter and a non-binding indicative financing term sheet from the Export-Import Bank of the United States (”EXIM”) for potential debt financing of up to $2 billion.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!X8hr!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F6235d04b-aa34-4840-ac85-889f344ef95e_1971x978.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!X8hr!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F6235d04b-aa34-4840-ac85-889f344ef95e_1971x978.png)

This is far more than a loan commitment. EXIM is an official export credit agency of the U.S. Federal Government. **Its involvement signifies that the Stibnite project, set to become the only domestic mined source of the critical defense material antimony, is deemed vital to U.S. national and economic security**. This is a geopolitical and strategic de-risking event of the highest order, providing a sovereign stamp of approval that dramatically reduces the political and permitting risk that has historically suppressed the stock’s valuation. This government backstop provides Perpetua with immense leverage in all other financing discussions, significantly lowering its future cost of capital and maximizing value for equity holders.

Second, on September 19th, the company received the critical Notice to Proceed from the U.S. Forest Service, authorizing the commencement of initial project development. This notice moves the project from a theoretical possibility to a tangible reality, clearing the final major administrative hurdle. With early works construction expected to begin this fall, the narrative has shifted permanently from “if” to “when,” forcing the market to re-price the equity accordingly.
