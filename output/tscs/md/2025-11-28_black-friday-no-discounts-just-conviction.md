### The Evolution: Welcome to TSCS

You may have noticed we’ve outgrown our name. While our roots are in the overlooked universe of small and medium-sized companies, our methodology applies universally.

We are moving away from “Small Cap Strategist” to simply **TSCS**.

Our focus isn’t defined by the size of the company, but by the gap between market perception and fundamental reality. TSCS provides institutional-grade research for discerning investors seeking a genuine edge, wherever that edge may be found.

We focus on:

* **Global Leaders and Megatrends:** Dominant players capitalizing on massive shifts, such as the AI infrastructure build-out, electrification, and deglobalization.
* **Contrarian Analysis:** Deep investigative research into controversial companies, identifying both compelling compounders and terminal zeros ignored by the consensus.
* **Complex Supply Chains and Commodities:** Unpacking under-covered markets like Copper, Cobalt, and rare earths, identifying the bottlenecks and the beneficiaries.

The advantage is found in the details the market ignores. That gap is where we operate, and it has never been more valuable.
